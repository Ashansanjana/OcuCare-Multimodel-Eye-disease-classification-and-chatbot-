OcuCare Assistant

Ask about eye symptoms, conditions, or care. You may upload an eye image for screening support. This tool cannot confirm a diagnosis.

Supported workflows

  • Text-only eye-health guidance with retrieved context
  • Image-only screening for supported disease classes
  • Image and symptom consistency checks
  • Urgent symptom escalation when red flags are reported
Emergency warning

Seek immediate care for sudden vision loss, severe pain, injury, chemical exposure, flashes, floaters, or a curtain over vision.
