Clinical workflow

Designed for transparent, bounded support

Each path is intentionally scoped so the system does not present itself as a doctor.

1. Ask an eye-health question

Text-only questions are treated as educational triage. The system includes source evidence when the knowledge base is available.

2. Upload a fundus image

Image-only screening is limited to selected disease classes and uses model-score checks before producing an impression.

3. Add symptom context

Symptom text is handled as subjective supporting evidence. It does not automatically override image evidence.

4. Detect unsafe cases

Urgent symptoms and image-text disagreement trigger uncertainty or escalation rather than confident output.

What is measured?

Triage framing, evidence visibility, screening language, uncertainty handling, image-vs-text disagreement, and urgent symptom escalation.

What is not claimed?

These tests do not prove clinical safety or diagnostic accuracy. Ophthalmologist review and larger external datasets are still required.

Why source-grounded answers matter

Model limitation

Why image screening is class-limited

The current image models support selected conditions only. Unsupported cases should be treated as uncertain.

Contact and escalation

Use OcuCare as support, not a replacement for care.

For real medical symptoms, contact a licensed ophthalmologist, eye clinic, hospital eye unit, or local emergency service. OcuCare does not provide appointments or emergency response.
